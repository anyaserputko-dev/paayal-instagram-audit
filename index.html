In short

Your content already does the hard part: you are on camera, your practices are clear, and people respond. Your median reel gets likes and comments from 0.99% of your followers, above the usual 0.36–0.55%.

What holds growth back is the road after the post. The bio does not say who you help, the free Clarity Call is hidden behind “and 4 more” and sits fourth on your Stan page, and none of the 31 posts since 1 August invites people to book.

My main proposal: keep your voice and your volume, and give every post one job, with a clear road to the call.

The numbers behind it

Reels reach mostly followers

Median 1,237 views across 85 reels, 29% of your follower count. Only 5 of 85 got more views than you have followers. Views count every play, not unique people. Instagram ranks reels on watch time, likes and sends, and sends matter most for people who don’t follow you yet (Adam Mosseri). So the reels to make more of are the ones someone forwards to a friend.

Engagement is healthy

Median (likes + comments) per reel is 0.99% of followers; carousels 0.71%, single photos 0.86%. Typical Instagram engagement by followers is 0.36–0.55% (Rival IQ, Socialinsider), and health and beauty sits at the low end. Engagement is not the problem; reach to new people and the path to the call are.

Formats

In 12 months: 85 reels, 37 carousels, 16 single photos. Reels are the format for reaching new people, carousels for saves and trust. In Buffer’s study of 4 million posts (2022–2024) carousels had the highest engagement by reach. Single photos lose reach in Metricool’s and Socialinsider’s data, so I would turn most of them into carousels.

Rhythm

11.5 posts a month on average, but uneven: 2 a month in January and February, 16–20 a month from May to August, 9 in September and 6 in October. Posting more is not the fix. A steady week where each piece has a job is.

What I propose

First 30 days

  • Week 1: everything from points 01–10. New bio and name field, links in order, Stan page with the call first, the Instagram link on your site and Stan fixed, highlight order and covers, three pinned posts, Direct questions, profile photo.
  • Content plan for the month around your real topics: stress and burnout, sleep, pranayama, meditation, yoga off the mat.
  • From week 2: the weekly rhythm below, with the call at the end of the pieces that solve a problem.

Every month

  • 8 reels: scripts you can read from a prompter, editing, subtitles in your style, covers, captions.
  • 4 carousels on your photos, like the two above.
  • 4 story series of up to 5 stories, plus simple everyday stories you can post yourself.
  • Monthly report: views, sends, saves, DMs, Clarity Calls and Stan downloads, what worked and what changes next month.

How Instagram feeds YouTube and Substack

  • Every talking reel also goes to YouTube Shorts, with a title written for search.
  • Once a month, one longer YouTube video (a guided practice or a topic in depth) that the next month’s reels are cut from.
  • Each week’s carousel topic becomes a Substack letter with your personal side of it and the Clarity Call link; stories point people to it.

How we measure it

  • Main number: Clarity Calls booked per week, and where they came from (one question on the Stan booking form).
  • Then: DMs started from stories and Direct questions, Stan downloads and sales, sends and saves per post.
  • Follower count is not a goal on its own; Instagram itself says views and likes matter more.
Your time: one or two filming sessions a month, up to 1.5 hours each, with scripts ready, and up to 15 minutes a week to approve.
